# Code of Conduct

Pharaonic projects expect respectful, professional collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific when reviewing code or ideas.
- Critique technical work, not people.
- Welcome good-faith questions and different experience levels.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Do not post spam, undisclosed promotion, or copied answers presented as verified without testing them.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, move, lock, or remove discussions, comments, or other participation that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

Report conduct concerns privately rather than escalating them in public threads. Use **Report content** from the menu on the post or comment.
